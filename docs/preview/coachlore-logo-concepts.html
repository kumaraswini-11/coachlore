Coachlore logo concepts

One thing to see before judging: the snake-draft path, shared by marks 3 to 5, reads as the digit "2" at small sizes, so the two marks meant to be Coachlore's own (1 and 2) don't use it.

The name, from ADR 0005: in a draft league the players are coaches, and lore is what a coach knows, every Pokémon, move and matchup (the Dex) and the league's history of picks, trades and replays. So the marks say "coach", "lore" or both, with the snake-draft order as the shared motif. Each is shown large, as a lockup with the name, at the real favicon sizes (16 to 48 px) on light and dark, and as an app icon and a maskable (circle-cropped) icon. Every mark is one colour, drawn on a 24 × 24 grid like the app's Lucide icons, so it follows the theme. The app's icon today (apps/web/app/icon.svg, a 3 × 3 draft grid) is a placeholder. No mark is round with a band across it: that reads as a Poké Ball, and AGENTS.md keeps "Poké" out of the name and the logo.

1 · Playbook Recommended

An open book with one play drawn across its pages: where it starts, and an arrow to where it goes, as in a coach's playbook. The name in one object: the coach, and the lore they keep. The book reads at 16 px; the play shows from 24 px.

2 · C monogram

A heavy C whose end is the first pick. The most legible at 16 px and the most ownable, the way an initial is; it says less of "coach" and "lore" than the Playbook does.

3 · Coach's clipboard

A coach's clipboard with the draft order on it. Says "coach" plainly and is easy to recognise, but it says nothing of the lore, and clipboards are a common icon for lists and tasks.

5 · League crest, for each league

A sports crest. Better as a system than as the product's logo: every league gets its own badge from the same shape, in its own colour with its initials (spec 001 leaves uploaded logos out: "a colour and initials do the job"). The product's mark sits beside it.

The recommendation: the Playbook as Coachlore's mark, since it's the only one that carries the whole name, with the C monogram as the alternative if the 16 px tab icon matters most. The League crest is each league's badge either way: one is the product, the other every league inside it. Judge them at 16 px first (the browser tab), then the app icon (a phone's home screen), then the big one: a logo that only works big isn't the one.
